// about

Typography is the art.

Checksum Error makes clothes for the people who write the software, train the models and get paged at 3 a.m. Every piece carries one idea, six words or three lines of code at most, set in monospace and printed in light ink on dark cotton.

No cartoon robots. No binary rain. No other company's logo on your chest. Just the inside joke, delivered deadpan, on a shirt you would actually wear outside the office.

Designed in Estonia. Printed to order, so nothing sits in a warehouse.
